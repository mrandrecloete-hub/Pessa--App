Refunds and cancellation

Version 1.1. Updated 4 October 2026.

We want you to be sure before you pay, so every new business starts with a free 14 day trial. This page explains how cancelling and refunds work once you subscribe.

1. Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time by simply not renewing. Nothing renews by itself and there is no cancellation fee
  • Your licence keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. After that Pesa locks, your records are kept, and you can still download a backup

2. When we refund

  • A payment made twice or by mistake is refunded in full
  • If a fault stops Pesa from working and we cannot fix it within 14 days of you telling us about it, we refund the unused full months of your current period
  • We also refund whenever the law requires it

3. When we do not refund

  • Once a licence key has been issued, payments for a period that has started are not refunded because you changed your mind, stopped using Pesa, or did not use all of the period. This is why the free trial exists
  • We do not refund a licence that was ended because these terms were broken

4. Upgrading and downgrading

  • You can move to a bigger plan at any time. You pay the difference for the rest of your period and we send a new key
  • A move to a smaller plan starts at your next renewal

5. How to ask for a refund

  • Send your Licence reference, the date of the payment, your proof of payment and a short note of what went wrong, by WhatsApp to 0818211692 or by email to ecrypted5@gmail.com
  • We answer within 5 working days. Approved refunds are paid back to the account or wallet that paid, within 10 working days
