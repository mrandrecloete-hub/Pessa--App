How to get your licence

No card, no sign up form. You pay by transfer or mobile wallet and we send a key.

  1. Try Pesa free for 14 days

    Open the app, register your shop and use every feature. Nothing is deleted when the trial ends.

  2. Find your Licence reference

    In Pesa open the menu, then Licence and subscription. Your reference looks like PESA-XXXX-XXXX and belongs to your shop only.

  3. Pay and send us the proof

    Pay the plan you chose by electronic transfer or mobile wallet. We will give you the payment details when you message us. Send your reference and proof of payment on WhatsApp.

  4. Paste your key

    We confirm your payment and send a licence key. Paste it in Licence and subscription and Pesa unlocks for the period you paid for.

Questions shop owners ask

Do I need internet?

No. Pesa works offline for selling, stock, credit and reports. You need internet to turn on online sync, to receive password reset emails and the first time you make a PDF.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Pesa asks for a licence. Nothing is deleted and the owner can still make a backup. When you send your key, Pesa opens again with everything as you left it.

What if I stop paying?

Your licence works until the end of the period you paid for, plus 7 days of grace. Then Pesa locks, but your records stay where they are. Renew when you are ready and carry on.

Does Pesa take card payments?

Pesa records card sales and adds them to your cash up, but it does not process card payments. You take card payments on your own card machine.

Is the VAT report official?

It is a working summary built from what you record. Check it with NamRA or your accountant before you file. It is not tax advice.

Which devices does it run on?

Any computer or device with a modern browser. You can install it to the home screen like an app.

Can I get my money back?

Yes, in the cases listed in our refunds and cancellation terms. That is why the free trial exists: try everything before you pay.
