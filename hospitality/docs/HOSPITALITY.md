# Pesa Hospitality Namibia: what is built

Everything runs on the device. Data is saved on the property's own phones and computers, with optional sync through the property's own Supabase project. Where something needs a server or an outside account, it says so.

## Built

| Need | Where in the app |
|---|---|
| Rooms, rates and types | Rooms |
| Bookings, deposits, double booking check, sources | Room bookings, New booking |
| Colour coded calendar for every room by night, with percent occupied, tap an empty night to book | Room calendar |
| Which rooms are free for dates and guests, grouped by type | Find a free room |
| Waiting list: guests wanting dates that are full, shown when a room frees up, offered on WhatsApp | Waiting list |
| Check in, check out, guest folio, charge at check out | Room bookings, tap a stay |
| Housekeeping: needs cleaning, being cleaned, ready, out of order with a note, who cleaned and when, arrivals first | Housekeeping |
| Tables and tabs, items for the kitchen and bar, a kitchen and bar board | Tables and tabs, Kitchen and bar board |
| Split bill (move chosen items to a new bill, share equally), merge two tabs | Tables and tabs, open a tab, Split bill |
| Recipes (ingredients per portion from the stock list), cost, margin, use as the cost price, food cost report | Recipes and food cost |
| Team clock in and out, hours per month, five criteria monthly ratings, hours and ratings with CSV | Team clock |
| Occupancy, average rate per night, revenue per available room, by room type, cancellations, CSV | Occupancy and rates |
| Guest messages for confirmation, day before, welcome, thank you and guest alerts, one tap each on WhatsApp | Guest messages |
| Guests list, licences and levy reminders | Guests, Licences and levy |
| Sales, till, VAT, invoices, receipts, expenses, stock, staff, payroll, reports, accountant pages, backups, Max assistant | The same Pesa engine as Pesa Retail Namibia |

## Not built yet, and why

- **Spa and shop with therapist appointments.** Not yet. The shop side already works as a product list and till.
- **Online booking page and online check in for guests.** These need a server or a hosted page that guests can reach. They are not in this version.
- **Two way connection to Booking.com, Airbnb or Expedia.** Needs each site's partner agreement and credentials. A calendar file (iCal) export and import is a good next step.
- **Taking ingredients off the stock list when a dish is sold.** Recipes work out cost and margin only.
- **Sending messages by itself.** Each message opens WhatsApp ready to send and you press send. Email and text messages from the app are not set up in this version.
- **Card payments are recorded, not processed.** As in Pesa Retail Namibia.
- **Room charges per night posted to the folio each night, rate plans, group bookings, auto assign.** Not built.
- **The assistant that books for you.** The local assistant (Max) is the Pesa one and does not make bookings.

Tax, levy, licence and records wording is to be confirmed with the relevant legal bodies, authorities and entities of Namibia. Nothing here has been reviewed by an accountant, auditor or security assessor.
