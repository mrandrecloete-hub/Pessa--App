Install Pesa

Put Pesa on your home screen.

Pesa installs straight from your browser in a few taps. It opens like an app, with its own icon, and keeps working offline.

  1. Open Pesa in Chrome with the button on this page.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. Pesa appears with your apps. Open it once with internet so it can save itself for offline use.
  1. Open Pesa in Safari. Other iPhone browsers may not offer install.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open Pesa from the home screen and let it finish loading once with internet.
  1. Open Pesa in Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac or Chromebook.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install Pesa.
  3. Pesa opens in its own window. Pin it to the taskbar or dock for one click access.

No store needed. Pesa runs in your browser, and installing only adds an icon.

Questions

Questions shop owners ask.

Do I need internet?

No. Pesa works offline for selling, stock, credit and reports. You need internet to turn on online sync, to receive password reset emails and the first time you make a PDF. Open Pesa once with internet so it can save itself for offline use.

How do I install Pesa?

Open Pesa in your browser and use the install option: on Android tap the three dots then Install app, on iPhone use Share then Add to Home Screen, and on a computer click the install icon in the address bar. No store is needed.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Pesa asks for a licence. Nothing is deleted and the owner can still make a backup. When you send your key, Pesa opens again with everything as you left it.

What if I stop paying?

Your licence works until the end of the period you paid for, plus 7 days of grace. Then Pesa locks, but your records stay where they are. Renew when you are ready and carry on.

Does Pesa take card payments?

Pesa records card sales and adds them to your cash up, but it does not process card payments. You take card payments on your own card machine.

Is the VAT report official?

It is a working summary built from what you record. Check it with NamRA or your accountant before you file. It is not tax advice.

Which devices does it run on?

Any computer or device with a modern browser: Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Chromebook. You can install it to the home screen like an app.

Can I use Pesa for a restaurant or a salon?

No. Pesa is a retail app: shops, spazas, wholesalers and general dealers.

Can I get my money back?

Yes, in the cases listed in our refunds and cancellation terms. That is why the free trial exists: try everything before you pay.
