Pesa Licence Issuer

Create licence keys for your customers. Everything happens on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and your private key never leaves this page.

1. Your signing keys

Do this once. The private key signs the licences. The public key goes into the Pesa app so it can check them. Keep the private key safe: anyone who has it can make valid licences.

2. Issue a licence

The customer opens Pesa, Menu, Licence and subscription, and sends you their Licence reference. Pay first, then issue.

3. Licences you have issued

Kept on this device only. Export it now and then so you have a copy.

4. Check a key

Paste any licence key to see what it says and whether your public key accepts it.
